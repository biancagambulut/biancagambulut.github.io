🌱 Why Data Analytics

I started my journey as a Civil Engineer, where I developed a strong analytical mindset, attention to detail and problem-solving abilities. Working in architecture helped me understand how systems, structure and technical decisions impact real-world outcomes.

While working with data inside engineering projects, I discovered that analytics is the part I enjoy the most: transforming raw information into clarity, visual insights and better decisions.

This inspired my transition into Data Analytics and Power BI development – a field where analytical thinking, design and business decisions meet. My goal is to combine my technical background with data storytelling to build meaningful dashboards that solve real problems.
